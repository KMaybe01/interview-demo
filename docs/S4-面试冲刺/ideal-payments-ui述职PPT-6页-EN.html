01 ｜ Business Overview

What does this system do?

The "money-purse hub" of corporate online banking — every corporate client transaction to pay out, collect, and manage money flows through this system.

→

Out · Payments

Remittances to suppliers (local / cross-border), transfers between company accounts, bulk salary payments, utility bills…
Once submitted, all instructions are centrally monitored in the Outbound Ledger.
Includes: payee directory · quick templates · recurring monthly auto-debits · payment stop & recovery (emergency risk block)
←

In · Collections

Collect receivables from customers one by one or in bulk collection runs; incoming funds all settle into the Inbound Ledger.
Includes: payer profiles · quick collection templates — maintain once, use everywhere, far less re-entry up front
✓

Control · Approval

Regulatory requirement: every transaction needs dual control — "maker + checker". One person submits, another approves, preventing unilateral control of funds.
Approvers can work in a central queue or in-context — tick multiple rows and batch approve / reject in one click
A transaction's full journey (payment example)
① Maker submits
Finance staff enters the payment instruction
→
② Pending approval
Money is not debited yet; it enters the review pool
→
③ Checker reviews
Approver approves / rejects
→
④ Settled
Approved = bank books & clears it
Rejected = reason recorded, fully traceable
Who uses it: corporate finance teams (submit) + approvers (review) ｜ End-to-end audit-ready
